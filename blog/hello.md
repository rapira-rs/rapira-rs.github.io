# Hello, Rapira

Welcome to the Rapira blog.

Today we're launching the documentation site along with this blog, so there's finally a place for release news, the reasoning behind our design decisions, and whatever else happens around the project.

Before that, on July 20, we opened up all of the Rapira repositories: the project now develops in full view of the community, under the [rapira-rs](https://github.com/rapira-rs) organization on GitHub.

Which means you can already:

- **star [the repository](https://github.com/rapira-rs/rapira)** — the simplest way to say the project matters to you, and it helps other people find it.
- **take a look at the [issues](https://github.com/rapira-rs/rapira/issues)** — ask a question, propose a feature, or weigh in on an issue someone else already opened. Early feedback shapes the API more than anything else does.
- **join our chat on [Discord](https://discord.gg/z7dcFATc9Q)**. But keep in mind that new functionality is proposed and discussed only in the issues.
- **watch the repository** if you'd rather just follow along and see where things are heading.
